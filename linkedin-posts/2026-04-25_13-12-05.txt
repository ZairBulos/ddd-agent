Bounded Contexts aren't just microservice boundaries. That's the oversimplification that often undermines DDD efforts.

Many developers treat a Bounded Context as a technical wrapper for a service or a module. This overlooks its core purpose: safeguarding *model integrity* within a specific area of the domain.

A true Bounded Context defines where a particular domain model, with its unique Ubiquitous Language and rules, holds sway. Consider 'Product' in an 'Inventory Management' context versus 'Product' in a 'Sales & Marketing' context. Same word, vastly different attributes, behaviors, and meaning.

Treating them as mere technical boundaries leads to diluted models and leaky abstractions. Your codebase won't accurately reflect the business reality, making evolution painful.

To do it right, immerse yourself in the domain's language. Map out the different meanings of key terms and how they behave across subdomains. This is how you identify meaningful Bounded Contexts that truly protect your domain models and lead to resilient systems. Stop oversimplifying; start modeling the business.
